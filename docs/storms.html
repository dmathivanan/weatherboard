Forecast columns are Open-Meteo's own archive of what it said one and three days ahead (raw, before bias correction), summed over the storm's days. Predicted applies the crest model to the rain that actually fell, so its error measures the model, not the forecast. Rows in red crossed the 6.5 ft notify line.

Column notes

Peak 10 min
The heaviest 10 minutes of the storm, shown as a rate (in/hr = inches in 10 min × 6). It is the rain term the sump model uses; the station reports every 5 minutes, so 10 minutes is the finest honest window.
Peak 1 h
The heaviest 60 minutes, in inches.
Wetness
Antecedent precipitation index at the moment the storm began (0.9/day decay), in inches.
Inflow
Water arriving at the sump pit, derived from the pump: seconds the primary pump ran in the busiest 15 minutes × 84 GPM rated capacity, averaged over that window. 84 is the ceiling; last winter's max was 42.65.
Runs
Primary-pump starts during the storm and the 12 h after it.
Run (this season)
The single longest primary-pump run, in seconds. Run length, not run count, is the signal that tracks load: stress = 1 − 14 / run_s.
Ross eq
The Ross gauge (USGS 11460000) reading that corresponds to the predicted Bridge Street crest, via bridge = 0.767 × ross − 1.96.
Error
Predicted crest minus actual crest. Red when outside the model's 95 % band.
